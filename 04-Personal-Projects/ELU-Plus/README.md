# ELU+ – Financial Life Simulation

ELU+ is an interactive financial literacy game developed at the **AI Eesti Youth Hackathon** by a team of three to address a challenge presented by **Coop Pank**.

The goal was to create an innovative solution that helps young people develop financial awareness and understand how everyday decisions affect their financial future.

### Key Features
- Interactive life simulation with real-world financial decisions
- Choices involving housing, cars, savings, investments and career
- Visualization of the long-term impact of financial decisions
- Unexpected life events and financial challenges
- An engaging and practical approach to financial education

### Technologies
- TypeScript
- React
- Vite
- Lovable (AI-assisted development)

### About the Project
Developed collaboratively with two teammates during the AI Eesti Youth Hackathon, ELU+ combines technology, financial education and business-oriented problem-solving.

The project provided hands-on experience in teamwork, rapid prototyping, AI-assisted development and transforming a real business challenge into a working digital prototype.
